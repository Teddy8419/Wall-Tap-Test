Wall tap test

Plays a short burst of sound into the wall at each spot and records how the wall answers. If the stud spots sound different from the hollow spots, the idea works.

How to run the test
  1. Pick a stretch of wall with at least two studs you can confirm, using outlet boxes, nail heads, or a regular stud finder.
  2. Lay a tape measure or painter's tape with 1-inch pencil ticks along it.
  3. Take off any thick case, turn the volume all the way up, and keep the room quiet.
  4. Tap Turn on microphone and stay quiet for a second while it measures the room.
  5. Press the back of the phone flat on the wall with its left edge on 0. Use the same firm palm pressure every time.
  6. Measure every spot, either by tapping or hands-free with beeps telling you when to move.
  7. Tap the yellow tape below at the spots where the studs really are.
  8. Run a second pass over the same stretch to see if the pattern repeats.

A good result: the boom line dips at every stud you marked, in both passes, and the dips land outside the gray noise band.
