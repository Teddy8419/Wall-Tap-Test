Stud method tester

Compares ways of holding and buzzing the phone, and scores which one tells a stud from a hollow spot most reliably.

Setup
  1. Find one spot you're sure is over a stud (nail heads, the side of an outlet box, or a regular stud finder) and one hollow spot 6 to 8 inches away. Mark them S and H with tape.
  2. Turn media volume and vibration intensity all the way up in the phone's settings, and keep the room quiet.
  3. Lift the phone off the wall and set it back for every reading. That's what shows whether a method is consistent.
  4. Test with the kind of case your customers are likely to have.

Cues

Spoken cues say "stud" or "hollow" before each reading. With beeps, two high beeps mean stud and one low beep means hollow.

Results

Each method is scored by how often it would label a reading correctly using only the other readings from that wall. Guessing gets about half right, marked by the line on each bar. One lucky wall can fool you, so confirm the winner on at least two more walls.
