Magnet stud finder

Finds the steel screws and nails that hold drywall to studs, using your phone's compass sensor.

Turn on the compass sensor for web pages

Chrome keeps this sensor off for web pages until you switch it on once.

  1. Copy this address:
  2. Open a new Chrome tab, paste it into the address bar, and go.
  3. Set Generic Sensor Extra Classes to Enabled.
  4. Tap Relaunch, then come back to this page.

iPhone browsers don't let web pages read the compass sensor, so this finder needs an Android phone with Chrome, or a native app.

How to scan
  1. Take off any case or wallet with a magnet in it.
  2. Find your phone's sensor spot: after tapping Start, slide a steel screw or spoon slowly over the back of the phone. Where the meter jumps highest is the sensor, usually near the top. That spot is the ring on screen.
  3. Hold the phone flat on a bare spot of wall and tap Start. Tap Reset whenever you move to a new area.
  4. Slide slowly sideways without turning the phone.
  5. When you get a hit, check a few inches above and below for more screws. A vertical line of hits is a stud.
  6. Scan at a few heights, since screws can be a foot or more apart.

This senses only steel and iron. It can't see wood, copper, plastic pipe, or wires, and a strong hit isn't always a screw. Check for wires and pipes another way before you drill.
